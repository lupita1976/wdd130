Our Trips

Whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned thrill-seeker, we have an adventure that fits you. Browse our most popular trips below, each one designed with safety, fun, and unforgettable memories in mind.

Family Float Trip

A relaxed, scenic trip perfect for beginners and families with kids. Enjoy gentle rapids (Class I–II) and calm stretches of river.

Duration: 2 hours

Equipment provided: Raft, paddles, life jackets, helmets

Classic Whitewater Adventure

Our most popular trip, combining exciting Class III rapids with beautiful canyon views. Great for first-time thrill-seekers.

Duration: 4 hours

Equipment provided: Raft, paddles, life jackets, helmets, wetsuit (optional)

Extreme Rapids Expedition

Built for experienced rafters chasing an adrenaline rush. Navigate Class IV rapids with our most experienced guides.

Duration: Full day

Equipment provided: Raft, paddles, life jackets, helmets, wetsuit, safety kayak escort

Overnight Camping & Rafting

Combine a full day of rafting with a night camping along the riverbank under the stars. Includes dinner and breakfast.

Duration: 2 days / 1 night

Equipment provided: Raft, paddles, life jackets, helmets, tents, sleeping bags, camping gear
